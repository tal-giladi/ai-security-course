# Module 14 quiz — RAG retrieval attack surface

This quiz covers the RAG retrieval attack surface: the difference between compromising the corpus and compromise the retriever, plus indirect injection through retrieved documents.
 It also covers denial of wallet — an untrusted tool that converts data it already holds into recurring, victim-billed inference cost.

Scenario questions, not recall: you are given a situation and asked what to do, what it costs, and what it does not stop. Pass mark **70%**.
