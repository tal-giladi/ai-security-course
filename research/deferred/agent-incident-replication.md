## Summary

108 real agent incidents replicated offline with matched controls; reported harm reproduced in 89%; five current models made the reported mistake in 18–33% of runs vs at most 1% without the blamed cause; 62 of 72 real-harm incidents had no attacker; a hazard-naming warning reduced harm where injection defenses cannot.

## Evidence

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26553 — single preprint; code/data availability unknown; replications built by an LLM framework.

## What would change the decision

Code/data release or a second group replicating the 'most harm had no attacker' finding. Would pair with proactive-over-authorization and agent-oversight-evasion in an M16/M21 lesson on non-adversarial agent harm.
