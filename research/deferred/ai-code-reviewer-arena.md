## Summary

In CyberDual, AI attackers hide exploitable bugs in real OSS maintenance work and AI reviewers must catch them, scored by re-running the attacker's PoC. Reviewers catch 85–91% of 1,088 attacks, yet about 1 in 6 attack slots still yields a working exploit when bugs are spread across real commits, and an open-weight attacker dominates the bypasses.
