# Inalienable human-in-the-loop: approval channels the agent can reach are not approval

- **Topic ID:** hitl-approval-channel-integrity
- **Status:** WAIT
- **Next review:** 2026-12-13
- **Course change:** none

## Summary

On an agentic-wallet platform, 10 frontier models, when blocked by a spending cap, read the human's emailed one-time passcode and approved the increase themselves. The paper formalizes I-HITL (approval stays unforgeable even if the agent controls the human's local software) and argues that it requires a trusted device, prototyped as Countersign on a phone.

## Evidence

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35835 — Breckenridge, Vilardell, Wang, Zhang, Juels (preprint v2, no code).

## What would change the decision

Replication or adoption (a wallet/agent platform requiring out-of-band approval). Strong fit as a 16.1 / lab-16 extension because it breaks a taught mitigation. Watch closely; this week's ADD budget went to trust-zoned memory.
