# Attack reuse and defense revision after disclosure (Tensor Trust evidence)

## Summary

In 563,349 Tensor Trust records, targets reused 10.0% of attacks that breached them within a week, versus 0.03% of matched non-breaching attacks. Same-family attacks became 24.5pp less successful against breached targets, and previously received attack text made up 23.2% of successful hijacks. The authors propose disclosure practices that describe the technique without releasing the full attack text.

## Evidence

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36422 — Chen, Zheng, Zhu (working paper).

Peer review or replication. It would be a short evidence-based disclosure note in 26.1. The course's own research/ pipeline already summarizes techniques rather than republishing payloads.
