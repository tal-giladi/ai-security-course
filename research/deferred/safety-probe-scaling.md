## Summary

Probe error floor follows a power law in model size and falls with labels at a size-dependent rate; laws fit on small models predict the next size within 0.02 in 72% of cases.

## Evidence

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30678 — single preprint (UW + Anthropic co-author), no code.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40588 — Pre-Crime for Agents: pre-generation activation probe for an agent about to give in to multi-agent peer pressure; single unaffiliated author, confounded by permitted disclosures, barely ahead of a bag-of-words baseline, no code. Added 2026-11-08.

## What would change the decision

Code/datasets or replication; the course has no probe-monitoring lesson yet, so this would need a placement decision (M23) first.
