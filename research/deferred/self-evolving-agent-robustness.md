# Self-evolving / continual-learning agents: robustness decay and misinformation absorption

- **Topic ID:** self-evolving-agent-robustness
- **Status:** WAIT

## Summary

Two code-released results: (1) personal agents that evolve skills or learn by RL from feedback become more susceptible to indirect injection, by +14.0 to +48.8pp and +3.0 to +39.3pp ASR respectively; (2) 'surprise'-driven continual learners absorb planted lies about known facts at roughly random-selection rates, and the best combined defense degrades when an attacker can plant matching sources.

## Evidence

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39122 — Evolve-IPI (Xiao lab), code github.com/SaFo-Lab/Evolve-IPI (not inspected).
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39840 — Metales & Grabenheinrich, code github.com/Metalesaek/sura-metales (not inspected).
