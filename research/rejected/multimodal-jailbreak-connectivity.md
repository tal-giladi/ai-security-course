## Summary

Optimizes continuous paths through low-loss regions of image space instead of isolated adversarial points. Reports 84–98% ASR on three open VLMs (MM-SafetyBench) and +25–27pp over FigStep in black-box transfer to frontier models.

## Evidence

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38091 — Zhang, Hu, Han, Wang (preprint, no code, no independent validation).

## What would change the decision

Reopen only if it becomes a standard baseline in independent evaluations or a defense paper, or if it is shown to defeat a defense the course teaches. The attack class (optimization-based adversarial images) is already taught in 06.1/20.1 and 09–10; this is a stronger optimizer, not a new concept.
